# Score/price exchange: policy v16

Budget requires at least 5% role-price savings per positive quality point lost; Balanced requires 10%. Fractional points scale continuously on the 0–100 role-quality scale. Each comparison uses the fixed initial selection for that role, before cost optimization. No swap-count or separate loss cap applies. Free replacements can therefore lose at most 20 points in Budget or 10 in Balanced.

Measured role scores and observed Intelligence v5 remain required for new replacements. Price exclusions, generations, diversity, coding independence and Heavy improvement remain enforced. Statistical Intelligence and CAI estimators remain disabled. Quality is unchanged. Costs are comparative adjusted eight-task cycle estimates.
