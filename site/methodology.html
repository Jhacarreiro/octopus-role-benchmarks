Role quality and portfolio modes

The detailed ranking table exposes role-quality signals and cost-per-quality values. The eight recommendation cards are generated separately as constrained portfolios: Quality, Balanced and Budget.

QualityIntelligence floor 0.85 → adaptive ≥5-family pool → maximize total Role Quality
Balancedexclude highest price level (all ties) → remaining latest-gen cost cutoff (mean + 2σ) → recompute best Intelligence → floor 0.80 → maximize Σ(75% quality percentile + 25% affordability percentile) → premium-placement pass within 1% of optimum
Budgetexclude two highest distinct price levels (all ties) → remaining latest-gen cost cutoff (mean + 2σ) → recompute best Intelligence → initial floor 0.75 → maximize role Budget Scores (40% quality / 60% affordability) → minimize cost within fixed role-score loss bounds

Balanced first excludes the highest distinct plan-adjusted task-price level; Budget excludes the two highest levels. Every offer tied at an excluded price is removed, using the published six-decimal USD precision. These levels come from the portfolio candidate universe before applying the Intelligence threshold. The mean and population standard deviation are then calculated from the remaining latest-generation scored models, and models above mean + 2σ are excluded too. Removed prices never contribute to these statistics. The best surviving Intelligence Index is recomputed before applying the configured 0.80/0.75 floor. If that pool cannot support a feasible portfolio, the existing adjustment lowers the floor in 0.005 steps; price exclusions stay enforced. Quality retains its unfiltered Intelligence baseline. Quality requires at least four families; Balanced and Budget require at least five. Every mode allows at most two seats per family.

The initial selection has no mandatory models, manual Fable blacklist, or per-role quality floor. The Budget cost pass adds explicit loss bounds relative to that initial selection. Code Reviewer must use a different model family from both implementation roles. Implementer Heavy must differ from Implementer and exceed its Implementer coding-quality score (same role-specific benchmark metric for both models) by at least 2.0 points in Quality, Balanced and Budget.

Budget cost optimization

After the initial Budget selection, a second pass searches for the cheapest complete portfolio with at most 1 point of loss per role on the 0–100 role-quality scale and at most 0.25 points of average positive loss across the eight roles. Improvements never cancel another role's loss. Every comparison uses that snapshot's original selection, never a preceding replacement. Changes require at least 10% total plan-adjusted cost savings.

There is no limit on the number of replacements below the initial Intelligence cutoff. New replacements require a directly measured role score and observed Intelligence v5; no Intelligence estimate is introduced. Price exclusions, generation rules, five-family diversity, family seat limits, coding independence and Heavy improvement remain enforced. Existing estimated seats may stay in the initial selection under their original cap; the second pass does not add estimated role replacements. An incomplete bounded search preserves the initial portfolio. Initial selections, losses, savings and Intelligence exceptions are recorded in the public lineup data.

Security Reviewer

Observed Security Reviewer = Artificial Analysis Cyber Index v1, directly. The previous SciCode//HLE/Omniscience/LCR proxy has been removed. Missing Cyber Index contributions use an explicitly labeled conservative role analogy; they never become measured Cyber Index observations.

Balanced and Budget calculate separate mean + 2σ cutoffs after their respective price-level exclusions. Balanced combines cohort-relative quality and affordability percentiles (75% / 25%), then may trade up to 1% of the optimum to place expensive models in planning/review and cheaper models in execution. Quality and Budget use that placement rule only as a tie-break; Budget maximizes role-specific 40% quality / 60% affordability scores, using cost as a tie-break.

CommandCode credit burn and normalized reference workload

Every model is repriced on the same versioned normalized reference workload. Model-specific task-token telemetry remains diagnostic and does not change this workload. All token prices, discounts, billing categories and Max allowances come exclusively from CommandCode.

task cost = fixed reference token profile × CommandCode input/cache/output prices

If CommandCode exposes no separate cache rate, normal input price is used. Active CommandCode promotions are retained.

CAI*: current observation, calibrated history, then estimator

If the current Coding Agent Index version has not re-evaluated a family, a prior observed CAI may be mapped onto the current scale with a validated overlap-derived degradation factor. Only families with neither current nor calibratable historical CAI use the estimator:

CAI* = 50% Ridge prediction + 50% inverse-distance 5NN prediction

Ridge (λ=1) uses SciCode, HLE, LCR, GDPval, AA-Omniscience Index (normalized to 0–100) and log(Output Tokens/Task). 5NN uses SciCode, HLE and LCR, standardized across the observed CAI training set, with Euclidean distance and inverse-distance weighting over the five nearest models.

The estimator is trained only on unique model families with current-version observed AA Coding Agent results. Calibrated historical values never train the estimator, and commercial CommandCode variants never count as extra training examples.

SciCode fallback: current observation first; otherwise a last observed value no older than 14 days may be used only after overlap calibration against current observations passes MAE/max-error guardrails. The calibration factor is capped at 1. Ridge is a third-line fallback and is usable only when its original leave-one-out guardrails pass; otherwise the family is source-incomplete.

Coverage and failure rules

Universal weighted-role components, CommandCode Cost per Task and final CAI* must cover the scored model-family universe according to the published coverage rules. Security Reviewer is role-specific and uses direct Artificial Analysis Cyber Index coverage instead. Stealth/unresolved rows remain visible as UNSCORED. Validation continues to fail closed when estimator/ranking guardrails are breached.

Raw model mappings, current/historical-calibrated/estimated CAI*, SciCode fallback provenance and calibration diagnostics, Ridge/5NN components, role scores, task costs and final Ranking Values are all preserved in the public JSON snapshot.

Portfolio regeneration

After refreshes, Octopus regenerates the complete Quality, Balanced and Budget portfolios under the current deterministic policy. Review data records portfolio-level changes rather than evaluating independent hand-picked seat swaps.

The policy file contains rules, not selected model names. Generated selections and their requested/effective Intelligence floors, pre/post-filter Intelligence baselines, family counts, costs and Balanced/Budget outlier statistics are published in site/data/lineups.json.
